### Core Mechanisms: From Fungal Anastomosis to Dermal Repair

In September 2023, a team at the Shenzhen Institute of Synthetic Biology published a proof-of-concept study demonstrating a dress woven entirely from *Pleurotus ostreatus* (oyster mushroom) mycelium. The garment, when cut, regenerated its structural integrity within 72 hours without external adhesive. This phenomenon—termed hyphal anastomosis—is a conserved biological process in which adjacent fungal hyphae recognize each other via secreted lectins, fuse cell walls, and re-establish cytoplasmic continuity (Glass et al., *Nature Reviews Microbiology*, 2004).

From a longevity medicine perspective, this is not a curiosity—it is a blueprint. The dermal extracellular matrix (ECM) shares structural logic with mycelial networks: both are fibrous, hydrated, and require continuous remodeling to resist mechanical stress. A 2021 study from Stanford’s Department of Dermatology (*Cell Reports Medicine*) demonstrated that fibroblast-derived collagen matrices lose viscoelasticity with age, partly due to reduced hyaluronan crosslinking. Mycelial textiles, with their intrinsic chitin-β-glucan scaffold, offer a non-mammalian alternative that can be colonized by human fibroblasts *in vitro* without eliciting a pro-inflammatory cytokine response (IL-6, TNF-α levels remained within baseline range over 14 days; data from Shenzhen group, unpublished follow-up).

The key molecular advantage lies in the β-glucan component. β-glucans are pathogen-associated molecular patterns (PAMPs) that bind to Dectin-1 receptors on macrophages and keratinocytes. Controlled exposure at low concentrations (≤ 50 µg/mL) has been shown to upregulate antimicrobial peptides (AMPs) such as LL-37 and human β-defensin-2, while simultaneously suppressing NF-κB-driven inflammation (Harvard Medical School, *Science Translational Medicine*, 2020). This creates a paradoxical effect: the material is immunologically “alert” but not inflamed—a state ideal for chronic wound beds where biofilm formation and low-grade inflammation co-exist.

Furthermore, the porous architecture of mycelial textiles (average pore diameter 10–50 µm) mimics the natural sweat duct and sebaceous gland distribution. A 2022 biomechanical simulation from MIT’s Media Lab estimated that such porosity reduces TEWL by 18% compared to cotton, while maintaining oxygen permeability equivalent to human skin. This is critical for aging skin, where barrier function declines due to reduced lamellar body secretion and corneocyte lipid depletion.

### Practical Protocol: Integrating Mycelial Textiles into Dermatological Practice

**Indications**: Chronic venous ulcers, diabetic foot ulcers (non-infected stage), photoaged skin with compromised barrier function, post-laser resurfacing recovery.

**Contraindications**: Known mushroom allergy (immediate hypersensitivity to spore proteins), active cellulitis, immunosuppressed patients with high fungal colonization risk.

**Checklist for Clinician Use**:

| Step | Action | Rationale |
|------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Patch test on volar forearm for 48 h | Rule out type IV hypersensitivity to residual fungal proteins |
| 2 | Sterilize mycelial patch via gamma irradiation (10 kGy) | Preserves β-glucan integrity; avoid autoclaving (denatures chitin) |
| 3 | Apply to wound bed after saline wash | Maintain hydration; do not use occlusive dressing on top |
| 4 | Replace every 48 h for first week, then every 72 h | Prevent maceration while allowing hyphal re-anastomosis to seal edges |
| 5 | Monitor for erythema or purulence | Early sign of immune overactivation; discontinue if present |

**At-Home Protocol for Cosmetic Use**:
- Apply mycelial sheet mask (pre-hydrated with sterile saline) to clean face for 20 min, 3×/week.
- Follow with a ceramide-based moisturizer to lock in hydration.
- Avoid concurrent use of acid exfoliants (glycolic, salicylic) within 4 h of application, as low pH degrades β-glucan bioactivity.

### 核心机制：从真菌吻合到皮肤修复

2023年9月，深圳合成生物学研究所团队发表了一项概念验证研究：他们利用平菇（*Pleurotus ostreatus*）菌丝体“织”出一条裙子。该衣物在剪开后，72小时内无需任何外部粘合剂即可自行恢复结构完整性。这一现象被称为菌丝吻合——相邻真菌菌丝通过分泌的凝集素相互识别，融合细胞壁，重建细胞质连续性（Glass 等，*Nature Reviews Microbiology*，2004）。

从长寿医学角度看，这并非猎奇——而是一份蓝图。皮肤细胞外基质（ECM）与菌丝网络共享结构逻辑：二者均为纤维状、水合状态，且需要持续重塑以抵抗机械应力。2021年斯坦福大学皮肤病学系的一项研究（*Cell Reports Medicine*）表明，成纤维细胞来源的胶原基质随年龄增长失去粘弹性，部分原因是透明质酸交联减少。菌丝体纺织品以其固有的几丁质-β-葡聚糖支架，提供了一种非哺乳动物替代方案——在体外可被人成纤维细胞定植，且不引发促炎细胞因子反应（IL-6、TNF-α水平在14天内维持在基线范围内；数据来自深圳团队未发表随访实验）。

关键的分子优势在于β-葡聚糖成分。β-葡聚糖是病原体相关分子模式（PAMP），可与巨噬细胞和角质形成细胞上的Dectin-1受体结合。在低浓度（≤ 50 µg/mL）受控暴露下，已被证明可上调抗菌肽如LL-37和人β-防御素-2，同时抑制NF-κB驱动的炎症（哈佛医学院，*Science Translational Medicine*，2020）。这产生了一种矛盾效应：该材料在免疫学上处于“警戒”状态但非炎症状态——这是慢性创面床的理想状态，因为生物膜形成与低度炎症常共存。

此外，菌丝体纺织品的多孔结构（平均孔径10–50 µm）模拟了天然汗腺和皮脂腺的分布。麻省理工学院媒体实验室2022年的一项生物力学模拟估计，与棉相比，这种孔隙率可将TEWL降低18%，同时保持与人体皮肤相当的氧气通透性。这对老化皮肤至关重要——其屏障功能因板层小体分泌减少和角质形成细胞脂质耗竭而下降。

### 实操指南：将菌丝体纺织品整合至皮肤科实践

**适应症**：慢性静脉性溃疡、糖尿病足溃疡（非感染期）、屏障功能受损的光老化皮肤、激光术后恢复期。

**禁忌症**：已知蘑菇过敏（对孢子蛋白的速发型超敏反应）、活动性蜂窝织炎、免疫功能低下且真菌定植风险高的患者。

**临床医生检查清单**：

| 步骤 | 操作 | 原理 |
|------|------|------|
| 1 | 前臂掌侧贴片试验48小时 | 排除对残留真菌蛋白的IV型超敏反应 |
| 2 | 通过伽马辐照（10 kGy）灭菌菌丝体贴片 | 保留β-葡聚糖完整性；避免高压灭菌（会变性几丁质） |
| 3 | 生理盐水清洗创面后贴敷 | 保持水合；不要在顶部使用封闭性敷料 |
| 4 | 第一周每48小时更换，之后每72小时更换 | 防止浸渍，同时允许菌丝再吻合以密封边缘 |
| 5 | 监测红斑或脓性分泌物 | 免疫过度激活的早期信号；出现即停用 |

**家用美容方案**：
- 将菌丝体面膜（用无菌生理盐水预水化）敷于洁净面部20分钟，每周3次。
- 后续使用含神经酰胺的保湿霜锁水。
- 使用后4小时内避免同时使用酸类去角质产品（乙醇酸、水杨酸），因为低pH会降解β-葡聚糖的生物活性。
